Ryan Berlin

A reclusive raconteur.

Hard yet soft, light yet heavy; routinely disenchanted, yet never for long.

Ryan is a philanthropist of small mercies, an unesteemed writer, and dilettante of oddities.

If the light slips, you’ll glimpse him flitting 'midst fey forests, soaring through bilious, reverb-soaked skies, or nestled in the cracks of the acrapolis — roaming its subfuscous streets, protecting ne’er-do-wells and ambitionists alike: an unsung vigilante to the putrescent profane, an abstruse hero to the hallowed Areopagites.
